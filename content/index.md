---
title: Welcome to the Encyclopedia Galactica
---

The Encyclopedia Galactica is the library of documentation for
the [Memphis Galaxy Simulation Group](https://memgalsim.space).

This library contains information on:

- An [[Onboarding Guide]] for new members to the MGS
- [[Galactic Astrophysics]], outlining the background theory and observational
  information.
- [[Simulation Code Internals]], breaking down how simulations work
- [[Initial Conditions]] to begin simulations
- A [[Hardware Guide]] describing the computers that the MGS group has access
  to, and explaining their usage and quirks
